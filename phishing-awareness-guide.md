# Phishing Awareness Guide

## What is phishing?
Phishing is a trick where someone pretends to be a trusted organisation or person in a message or website to persuade someone to reveal private information, open an unsafe attachment or visit a harmful link.

## Warning signs
- The sender address or phone number looks unusual.
- The message creates pressure, fear or urgency.
- It asks for a password, PIN, payment or verification code.
- The link destination does not match the organisation's real website.
- The message contains an unexpected attachment or offer that seems too good to be true.

## How to stay safe
1. Pause before responding to an unexpected message.
2. Check the sender using a separate, trusted contact method.
3. Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.
4. Visit the organisation's official website by typing its address yourself.
5. Use a unique password for each important account.
6. Turn on multi-factor authentication.
7. Keep devices and applications updated.
8. Report suspicious messages to the official IT/helpdesk team.

## If you think you received a phishing message
Do not reply or forward it to friends. Use your organisation's approved reporting process. If you entered a password, contact the official support team promptly and change the affected password using the genuine website or app.

## Remember
**Pause. Check. Protect.**
