# Campaign Message

## THINK BEFORE YOU CLICK
### Pause. Check. Protect.

A message may look real and still be a trick. Before you click:
- Check who sent it.
- Be careful with urgent requests.
- Do not open unexpected links or attachments.
- Never share your password or verification code.
- Report suspicious messages to the official IT/helpdesk team.

**See it? Don't click it. Report it.**
